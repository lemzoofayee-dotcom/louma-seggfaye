Authentic Senegalese food, from the Îles du Saloum to your kitchen

I am Lamine Faye, known as the Guedjologue. I grew up in the Îles du Saloum, the Saloum Delta in Senegal, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Louma by Seggfaye brings you the ingredients of Senegalese cooking that you can't find in supermarkets: guedj (dried fermented fish), yeet (dried cymbium), netetou (dawadawa), dried shrimp, bissap, baobab, millet.

Why Louma

  • Direct sourcing from the fishermen and the women processors of the Îles du Saloum, with no middlemen.
  • Traditional methods: sun-dried, smoked or fermented the way it has always been done.
  • Responsible fishing: for example, we only sell yeet over the 17.5 cm legal size.

Shipping: our online shop ships across France and Europe. We also ship to the Americas (USA, Canada…), the UK and Asia: send us a message on WhatsApp and we will give you a shipping quote. We do not ship to Africa.
