African Christmas food: what Senegal eats at Christmas and New Year

By Lamine Faye, the Guedjologue Published November 17, 2026 Reading time: 3 min

In short: in Senegal, people of every religion celebrate Christmas. The festive table is built around roast chicken or méchoui with a sauce, raw-vegetable salads and fries, with soft drinks, fruit juices, bissap and homemade cakes. New Year's Eve brings almost the same meal, then dancing and fireworks at midnight.

I am Lamine Faye, the Guedjologue, and I grew up in the Îles du Saloum. When people search for African Christmas food, they often expect one single menu. Here is how it looks from Senegal, and how the diaspora brings it to Europe.

Christmas in Senegal: a celebration for everyone

In Senegal, Christmas is not only for Christian families: people of every religion celebrate it. Neighbours, friends and families share the same table.

The Christmas table

  • Roast chicken or méchoui (roasted meat), served with a sauce.
  • Raw-vegetable salads (crudités) and fries on the side.
  • Drinks: soft drinks, fruit juices and bissap, the hibiscus drink.
  • Homemade cakes for dessert.

Fish and seafood, the everyday stars of Senegalese cooking, are usually set aside for the holidays: at Christmas, meat takes the centre of the table.

New Year's Eve: the same table, then the party

On December 31, the meal is almost the same as at Christmas. Then the night goes on with dancing, and fireworks light up the cities at midnight.

In Europe: be creative

Far from home, the festive table can mix both worlds. A good thieboudienne with kéthiakh or guedj makes a festive dish that tastes like Senegal. And before the holidays, what the diaspora orders most from us is bouye (baobab powder) and red bissap: the drinks of the festive table.

Frequently asked questions

What do Senegalese people eat at Christmas?

Roast chicken or méchoui (roasted meat) with a sauce, served with raw-vegetable salads and fries. To drink: soft drinks, fruit juices and bissap. And homemade cakes for dessert.

Do Muslims celebrate Christmas in Senegal?

Yes. In Senegal, people of every religion celebrate Christmas. It is a family celebration shared by everyone.

What do Senegalese people eat on New Year's Eve?

Almost the same meal as at Christmas. Then the night goes on with dancing, and fireworks light up the cities at midnight.

Is fish or seafood part of the Christmas meal in Senegal?

Not usually. Fish and seafood are set aside for the holidays: meat is the star of the table. In Europe, you can be creative and serve a festive thieboudienne with kéthiakh or guedj.

Which Senegalese ingredients does the diaspora order before Christmas?

Mostly bouye (baobab powder, for baobab juice) and red bissap (dried hibiscus, for the bissap drink): the drinks of the festive table.
